## Which license fits your Mac, and who should skip Permute

The version question is decided by your hardware, not your preferences.

**Apple Silicon plus macOS 26 or later:** buy Permute 4. Nothing else applies.

**Intel Mac, or macOS 11 through 15:** you need Permute 3. Permute 4 will not run on your machine at all. Buying Permute 3 comes with a free upgrade to Permute 4 for when you eventually move to macOS 26, and the two versions do not share serial numbers, so don't buy one expecting the key to work on the other. One thing to watch: the store listing I looked at is for Permute 4, so if you're on an older system, confirm which version the key is for before you pay.

**Windows:** no version of Permute exists for it. There's no workaround worth chasing.

**Converting a file once a month:** skip it. QuickTime, Preview and HandBrake cover the occasional job, and Permute's advantage only shows up when you're converting batches, dealing with awkward formats like AV1 or HEIC, or want trimming and subtitle work in the same window as the conversion. Nobody needs a license to change one MP4.

**Needing PDF compression presets, AI upscaling, or table and font conversion:** skip it too. Those are gaps in Permute's feature set, and a competing app markets specifically against them.
